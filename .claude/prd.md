## 1. Executive summary

Construx is a multi-tenant SaaS platform designed to help construction companies manage their projects, workforce, tasks, finances, and operational information from a centralized application.
The platform aims to improve operational visibility, workforce coordination, project tracking, and information management across multiple construction sites.
This project focuses exclusively on building the Construx marketing landing page. The landing page will communicate the product's value proposition, present its core capabilities, and encourage potential customers to explore the platform or express interest in using it.
The landing page will be a responsive web experience accessible from desktop computers, tablets, and mobile devices.
The Construx platform is intended for construction teams working across Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android. The availability of dedicated applications for these platforms remains subject to future product decisions.

## 2. Product vision

> **Vision:** Provide construction companies with a centralized digital platform to coordinate their projects, workforce, and operations efficiently.

Construx aims to connect the different areas of construction management within one ecosystem.

### Core product areas

- **Project Management:** Organize construction projects, track progress, and manage project information.
- **Workforce Management:** Organize employees, define responsibilities, and assign workers to projects.
- **Task Management:** Create tasks, assign responsibilities, establish priorities, and track completion.
- **Construction Plans:** Associate activities with specific locations or areas on construction plans.
- **Project Scheduling:** Visualize project timelines, dependencies, and planned activities using Gantt charts.
- **Financial Management:** Organize construction-related expenses, purchases, and financial information.
- **CRM:** Manage customer relationships and relevant business information.
- **Multi-Tenant Organizations:** Allow multiple construction companies to use the platform while keeping their data isolated.

These capabilities describe the broader product vision. They do not need to be implemented as functional modules in this landing page project.

## 3. Problem statement

Construction companies face the following problems:

- **Lack of visibility:** Limited and untimely insight into what is happening at each construction site.
- **Poor workforce coordination:** Difficulty organizing workers and assigning them to projects.
- **Lack of task tracking:** Limited control over pending tasks, responsibilities, and progress.
- **Disconnected plans and operations:** Difficulty identifying which activities are being performed in specific areas of a construction site.
- **Lack of traceability:** Difficulty tracking who made a change, when it happened, and why.
- **Difficulty scaling:** Increasing complexity when managing multiple projects and workforces.
- **Lack of centralized control:** No unified platform connecting companies, projects, workers, and activities.
- **Lack of activity context:** The need to associate each task with a specific plan, Gantt chart, and its corresponding specifications.

## 4. Product goals

The landing page must:

- Clearly communicate what Construx is and who it is designed for.
- Explain the main problems the platform aims to solve.
- Present the core product capabilities in an understandable way.
- Demonstrate how Construx can improve construction project coordination.
- Establish a professional, trustworthy brand identity.
- Encourage visitors to take a meaningful next step.
- Provide a responsive experience across supported screen sizes.
- Establish a foundation for future marketing pages and product expansion.

## 5. Scope

### 5.1 In scope

- Responsive marketing landing page.
- Construx branding and visual identity.
- Product introduction and value proposition.
- Presentation of core product capabilities.
- Product interface mockups or illustrative previews.
- Primary and secondary calls to action.
- Navigation between landing page sections.
- Basic SEO metadata.
- Accessibility and performance considerations.
- Analytics integration readiness, subject to implementation decisions.
- Static or locally defined demonstration content.

### 5.2 Out of scope

- Backend development.
- User authentication and account registration.
- Organization creation or tenant management.
- Database integration.
- Live project data.
- Functional workforce or task management.
- Functional financial management or CRM.
- Real-time worker tracking.
- Interactive construction-plan editing.
- Functional Gantt scheduling.
- Subscription payments or billing.
- Production mobile applications.
- Administrative dashboards.

## 6. Landing page requirements

### 6.1 Header and navigation

The header must include:

- Construx logo or wordmark.
- Navigation links to relevant page sections.
- A primary call to action.
- Responsive mobile navigation.

#### Suggested navigation

- Product
- Features
- Benefits
- About
- Contact

Final navigation labels may change according to the content strategy.

### 6.2 Hero section

The hero section must immediately explain the product.

#### Suggested headline

> **Take Control of Every Construction Project.**

#### Suggested supporting text

> Manage projects, coordinate your workforce, track activities, and organize construction operations in one centralized platform.

#### Required elements

- Main headline.
- Supporting description.
- Primary call to action.
- Secondary call to action, if appropriate.
- Product visual, dashboard mockup, or construction-related illustration.

The hero section should communicate the product's value without requiring visitors to scroll.

### 6.3 Problem and solution section

The section should explain the main challenges construction companies face and how Construx aims to address them.

#### Potential topics

- Fragmented project information.
- Poor workforce coordination.
- Limited task visibility.
- Manual reporting.
- Difficulty managing multiple construction sites.

The content should focus on customer outcomes rather than technical implementation details.

### 6.4 Features section

The section should present the main capabilities of the broader platform.

#### Project Management

- Centralized project information.
- Project progress visibility.
- Organization of multiple construction sites.

#### Workforce Management

- Employee organization.
- Project assignments.
- Roles and responsibilities.

#### Task Management

- Task assignment.
- Priorities and deadlines.
- Progress tracking.

#### Construction Plans

- Visual representation of construction areas.
- Association between plans, locations, and activities.

#### Scheduling

- Project timelines.
- Gantt chart visualization.
- Task dependencies and deadlines.

#### Financial Management

- Expense and purchase organization.
- Project-related financial visibility.

#### CRM

- Customer and business relationship organization.

Each feature should include a concise explanation and an appropriate visual when useful.

### 6.5 How it works

The section should explain the intended product workflow at a high level.

#### Suggested steps

1. **Create Your Organization:** Establish the company's workspace.
2. **Organize Your Projects:** Create and manage construction projects.
3. **Coordinate Your Team:** Organize workers and assign responsibilities.
4. **Track Activities:** Monitor tasks, schedules, and progress.
5. **Improve Visibility:** Review relevant operational information in one place.

This section is explanatory and does not require a functional onboarding flow.

### 6.6 Product visuals

The landing page should use visuals to help visitors understand the product.

#### Potential visuals

- Dashboard mockup.
- Project overview.
- Workforce management interface.
- Task list or task board.
- Construction plan with activity markers.
- Gantt chart.
- Financial overview.

Mockups should look consistent with the proposed product experience.

Any fictional data must be clearly treated as demonstration content.

### 6.7 Benefits section

The section should explain the expected benefits of adopting Construx:

- Improve visibility across projects.
- Reduce dependence on fragmented tools.
- Improve workforce coordination.
- Make task responsibilities clearer.
- Centralize operational information.
- Support more consistent project monitoring.
- Build a foundation for managing multiple construction sites.

Avoid unsupported quantitative claims such as specific percentage improvements unless they can be validated.

### 6.8 Calls to action

The page must include clear opportunities for visitors to express interest.

#### Suggested primary CTA

> **Get Early Access**

#### Suggested secondary CTA

> **Explore Features**

The final CTA destination depends on whether a contact form, waitlist, or product demo process will be available.

Since backend functionality is out of scope, CTAs must not lead to a nonexistent registration or authentication system.

### 6.9 Footer

The footer should include:

- Construx branding.
- Navigation links.
- Contact information, when available.
- Privacy policy and terms links, when published.
- Copyright information.

Do not publish placeholder legal links as if they were completed documents.

## 8. Design and UX Requirements

- Use a professional visual identity appropriate for construction management software.
- Prioritize clarity, hierarchy, and readability.
- Use consistent spacing, typography, colors, and component styles.
- Use construction-related visuals without making the interface feel cluttered.
- Make calls to action visually distinguishable.
- Avoid excessive animation or distracting transitions.
- Ensure that product mockups remain legible on smaller screens.
- Maintain consistent branding throughout the page.
- The design should communicate reliability, organization, operational control, and modern technology.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Frontend

- The landing page will be implemented as a frontend-only application.
- The final framework and libraries must be confirmed before implementation.

#### Potential considerations

- React and TypeScript.
- Astro with React components, if static-first delivery is preferred.
- CSS modules, Sass, or another maintainable styling approach.
- Use Scroll based animations
- Use gsap/Motion.
- CReate something creative and unique.

The final stack should prioritize maintainability, performance, and the requirements of the landing page.

### Responsive design

The page must support:

- Desktop computers.
- Tablets.
- Mobile phones.

Layouts, navigation, typography, and visual elements must adapt to different viewport sizes.

### Performance

- Optimize images and other static assets.
- Avoid unnecessary JavaScript.
- Minimize layout shifts.
- Use responsive image sizing.
- Keep animations lightweight.
- Aim for strong Core Web Vitals performance.

### Accessibility

- Use semantic HTML.
- Support keyboard navigation.
- Provide appropriate alternative text for meaningful images.
- Maintain sufficient color contrast.
- Provide accessible labels for interactive elements.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### SEO

- Provide a descriptive page title and meta description.
- Use a logical heading hierarchy.
- Include appropriate social-sharing metadata.
- Use descriptive URLs if additional pages are introduced.
- Optimize images and page content for discoverability.
